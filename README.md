<h1>Spiceworks – How To Escalate A Ticket</h1>


<h2 style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif; font-size: 20px; font-weight: bold; margin-top: 24px; margin-bottom: 12px;">
⏹️ Description</h2>

<p style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 16px; margin-top: 12px; margin-bottom: 12px;">
In this project, you will use the Spiceworks ticketing system to escalate a user-reported issue that requires higher-level intervention. You will escalate a support ticket within Spiceworks by reassigning it to an appropriate higher-level IT team member or group. You access the Spiceworks ticketing system, select the relevant ticket from the ticket list, and open the ticket detail panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen. From the ticket interface, you navigate to the Attributes section on the right side, select the Assignee field, and choose the appropriate higher-level technician or team from the available list. The ticket will be formally escalated within the system and reassigned to the correct support level.
